# Market and competition

**Research date:** 8 October 2026. **Proposed decision:** NARROW. **Confidence:** moderate that a bounded experiment is justified; low that a sustainable business or unmet need has been demonstrated.

The [source register](research/sources.md) records dates and limitations. VERIFIED FACT is source-supported; COMPANY CLAIM is advertised; OBSERVED PUBLIC PAGE SNAPSHOT is retrieved content. None implies installed testing. Forecasts and thresholds are ASSUMPTIONS or INFERENCES.

## 1. The customer and job to test

**Recommended segment:** Pakistani personal desktop/laptop shoppers comparing two sellers for a **new branded wireless mouse** within 30 days. Recruit around an actual shopping occasion. Computer use does not imply shopping use or permission to install at work.

**Core job:** “Help me tell whether these offers are for the same mouse, understand what I would pay and what remains unverified, and keep the evidence while I decide.”

The category is a learning boundary, not a market-size claim. Manufacturer model numbers make disciplined matching plausible, while a small catalogue limits maintenance. Yet even Logitech M650 has standard, large and large-left variants; color, receiver bundle, region, condition and warranty may also matter. A shared family name is insufficient. [M31](research/sources.md)

The first experiment should test an evidence card and local comparison notebook, using synthetic or permission-cleared examples and user-entered facts. Automatically extracting or publishing retailer information remains governed by [data access](02-data-access.md). No reviewed store is preapproved merely because a page was readable. Keyboards, headsets, laptops, smartphones and fashion remain outside this first category.

A later mobile page could display manually supplied or licensed offer evidence; it could not inspect another app's selected product. [M29](research/sources.md)

## 5. Completed public checks and remaining audit limits

One purposively selected Farq router page was opened as an additional **public-content consistency check**, outside the proposed mouse category. It promoted PakByte PKR25,200 as its best price while its own availability table marked that offer out of stock and EezePC PKR29,990 in stock. This is an OBSERVED PUBLIC PAGE SNAPSHOT discrepancy in retrieved text. It is not a confirmed checkout mismatch, a test of current JavaScript behavior or an overall error rate. [M33](research/sources.md)

A separate [read-only interactive audit](research/public-audit.md) examined three mouse comparison surfaces and followed two retailer links. Farq/TechArc both displayed PKR7,000 for the selected M650 Graphite; Flashi/Naheed both displayed PKR8,930 for the selected white M650. Full variant, delivery and checkout equivalence were not established. Farq's M185 page promoted a best price while both listed offers were out of stock; Flashi marked the white M650 in stock while Naheed showed out of stock. These bounded observations are useful counterexamples, not an overall accuracy rate. Related models in search results are not automatically exact-match errors.

A reproducible next accuracy task uses M650, M650 L, M185, M331 and M240 queries, then records exact offered SKU, color, handedness, condition, warranty, seller, price components, stock and check time. M650/M650 L is an intentional near-match trap. Label every candidate “equivalent,” “different” or “insufficient evidence”; do not count abstentions as correct matches. Fix search order and record missing results. Five queries are exploratory, not enough to estimate market-wide precision.

## 6. Constraints an honest offer comparison must expose

| Constraint | Design consequence |
|---|---|
| Delivery and destination | Shophive's inspected offer uses Lahore-scoped delivery wording. Ask for coarse destination only when necessary; unknown delivery stays unknown. [M30](research/sources.md) |
| Price eligibility | The same page has a full item price and a smaller installment amount. Preserve payment conditions; never rank monthly repayment as purchase price. [M30](research/sources.md) |
| Seller, warranty and condition | Store identity and seller identity need separate fields on a marketplace. Record the specific offer's warranty assertion and provenance; an asserted warranty is not independently fulfilled or verified. New/used/refurbished offers never silently merge. |
| Model and variant | Manufacturer and retailer identifiers are different namespaces. Record size/handedness/color and receiver bundle; abstain when missing information could change equivalence. [M30–M31](research/sources.md) |
| Handset PTA status | FBR describes DIRBS compliance and blocking on mobile networks. If handsets are ever added, require explicit status evidence and fresh regulatory research. Do not treat a low non-comparable handset price as savings. [M32](research/sources.md) |
| Frequency and trust | Monetary savings may be small or infrequent for mice. Test whether reduced checking effort or avoided variant mistakes merit installation. Neither this value nor willingness to pay is established. |

## 7. Three entry strategies and a decision

| Strategy | Differentiation and potential defensibility | Disadvantage; cheapest falsification |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Exact-offer evidence notebook — recommended** | User-invoked local card highlights known facts, missing costs and equivalence; no account needed. Eventually defend through a reviewed variant dictionary, permission-cleared provenance and reliable correction process. | Manual entry may add more work than it saves; local operation alone is not a moat. In 10–12 observed tasks, compare against the shopper's existing tabs/notes; stop if it rarely changes understanding or saves effort. |
| **B. One merchant's permitted history companion** | Written rights and a well-defined feed can create genuine history and stock-aware watches for a bounded catalogue. | One store cannot establish cheapest-market prices; merchant interest is unproven. Seek one written, field-specific pilot agreement before adapter work; inability to secure rights disproves the access hypothesis. |
| **C. Two-store exact-match service** | Fresh, permissioned, comparable offers with transparent costs could displace manual tab comparison. | Requires two viable sources and enough overlapping mouse SKUs; stronger existing competition. First establish rights and a manually reviewed overlap table. Stop if fewer than ten suitable overlapping variants or no clear task improvement. |

Alternatives include retailer wishlists/alerts, tabs, search, community advice, buying locally and postponement. The extension earns adoption only if observed tasks show clearer decisions with less work. Validate that benefit and sustainable access before broadening the product or claiming savings.
