# Validation and growth

**Status:** PROPOSED research instruments, not founder approval to recruit, record, install or launch. **Results:** no interviews, participant tasks, installs, purchases, verified savings or willingness-to-pay tests have been executed. Public-source checks are recorded separately in [research progress](research/progress.md).

Test whether Pakistani personal desktop/laptop shoppers considering a **new branded wireless mouse** benefit enough to change their behavior. Device availability and competitor presence do not validate this. [M24–M29](research/sources.md) Follow [product requirements](03-product-requirements.md) and [data access](02-data-access.md).

## 1. Questions and evidence that would change the decision

| Hypothesis | Evidence required | Evidence that weakens it |
|---|---|---|
| Comparing mouse offers creates a meaningful problem | Recent concrete example; observed ambiguity, repeated work or unresolved cost | Shopper already resolves the task easily; savings/effort too small |
| A popup notebook improves the decision | Correct answer or actionable missing fact, with less work than normal behavior | Manual entry introduces errors or more work |
| Desktop is a useful intervention point | Participant normally performs this task on a personal computer | Shopping occurs in mobile apps; computer is unavailable or managed |
| An eligible merchant can supply useful facts | Scoped written grant, sample and necessary fields | Only an affiliate link, incomplete fields or forbidden retention |
| Use repeats when needed | Voluntary reuse on a second relevant shopping occasion | Only researcher-led use, sale-day curiosity or reminders produce activity |
| A business can fund reliable operation | Approved revenue inputs and measured operating/maintenance costs | Positive margin exists only with unconfirmed commissions or unpaid unlimited labor |

## 2. Recruitment and consent — NOT SENT

**Initial channel:** one Pakistani computing community permitting research invitations; read its rules and obtain required moderator permission. No membership is assumed. Recruit adults using a personal computer, considering a mouse purchase within 30 days and comparing two sellers.

Seek 10–12 sessions, including three extension-skeptical/manual-comparison users if possible. Avoid a friends/developers-only sample. Record recruitment composition and normal versus sale periods; claim no representativeness.

### Recruitment draft

> I am studying how people in Pakistan compare offers for new wireless mice. If you are 18 or older, use a personal laptop/desktop, and are considering a purchase in the next month, would you take part in a 25-minute research session? We will look at your usual comparison process and a proposed offer notebook. You do not need to install anything, buy anything or share account details. There is no payment for participation. Critical feedback is welcome. Contact [RESEARCH CONTACT]. This is research, not a product endorsement or sales offer.

### Screening questions

1. What are you considering buying, and when do you expect to decide?
2. Which device would you normally use to research and purchase it? Is that computer yours, or managed by an employer/school?
3. Which stores or other sources have you already checked?
4. Have you used a comparison tool or extension for this task? Which one, if any?
5. Are you at least 18, and comfortable taking part without payment?

Collect no income, identity documents or precise addresses. Use fictional cities for synthetic delivery tasks.

### Consent script

> Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop without explaining why. Please close private tabs and do not show passwords, checkout, messages or payment details. I will record task outcomes and brief notes under a research ID. Audio/video recording is optional and will occur only with your separate agreement. Identifiable notes and recordings will be deleted within 30 days of the session; redacted findings may be kept for 90 days. Until de-identification, contact [CONTACT] with your research ID to request deletion. Your comments will not be published as a testimonial. May we proceed with note-taking? Separately, do you agree to recording?

Record consent choices separately; recording defaults off. Keep scheduling contacts separate and outside public repositories. Replace placeholders and verify storage/deletion arrangements before recruitment. Research retention is distinct from extension retention.

## 3. Non-leading interview: approximately ten minutes

Use “Can you give an example?” and “What happened next?” without suggesting a problem.

1. “Tell me about the last mouse or similar accessory you researched. Start where you first decided you needed it.”
2. “What did you check before choosing where to buy?”
3. “How did you keep track of the options, if at all?”
4. “What made you believe two offers were the same item?”
5. “What happened when a price, warranty or delivery detail was missing?” Ask only about ambiguities the participant actually encountered; otherwise use “Was anything unclear?”
6. “How did you decide you had checked enough?”
7. “Did you buy, postpone, choose a different item or do something else? What influenced that?”
8. “Which tools did you use? What made you keep or stop using them?”
9. “For the purchase you are considering now, what will you do next?”

Record behavior and examples. “I would probably use it” is interest, not activation. Do not suggest competitors are inaccurate or fraudulent.

## 4. Observed task: baseline and proposal

**Preparation:** create two equally complex, labeled synthetic offer sets: model, price, delivery, warranty and stock, including a near-match, missing charge and unavailable offer. Seek a second answer-key reviewer; otherwise disclose founder-only labeling. Real merchant fixtures require access review. [D01–D03](research/sources.md)

**Task A — usual approach:** “You want [specified model, size, color and condition] delivered to [fictional city]. Use your normal method to decide which offer you would investigate next, or explain why you cannot decide. Think aloud if comfortable.” Let the participant use tabs, paper or notes. Start timing after the task is understood. Do not correct them while they work.

**Task B — proposed notebook:** explain starting controls, then: “Work through this set using the notebook. Decide what to do next and explain the evidence.” Counterbalance fixture/method order. Use a mockup or reviewed prototype on the founder's device; the participant performs entries. If the founder operates controls, label the task mediated and exclude direct-use timing claims. Demonstrations are not installs.

**Probe:** “What differed?” “What did you trust, and why?” “What was unnecessary?” “What would you change?” “Without this tool, how would you finish?” Investigate misunderstood English/Urdu/Roman Urdu terms before deciding translation is needed.

Grade against the answer key: exact identity, condition/warranty distinctions, full versus conditional price, missing delivery and stock. A justified “insufficient evidence” can be the correct task outcome. Record critical false-equivalence separately. M650 versus M650 L is a useful real taxonomy reference, not interchangeable fixture data. [M31](research/sources.md)

It includes no customer file-import feature or hosted ingestion. Hosted catalog/API work remains conditional P2. An inquiry or logo is not a grant. [D01–D09](research/sources.md)

A public P2 single-merchant explainer may proceed only if its own value and release gates pass. Cross-store tasks additionally require two independently eligible merchant/source integrations with exact overlapping offers; two sellers on one marketplace do not by themselves satisfy that requirement.

Competitor checks use five fixed mouse queries and search order. Record time, source type, variant, missing results, comparable candidates, age and costs against contemporaneous permitted evidence. Separate retrieved text, interactive websites and installed behavior. Report blockages/abstentions; search misses are not functional failures. Desk checks are not a matching benchmark.

After review and separate authorization, measure a private-build funnel: invited → consented → install attempted → installed → opened → accurate useful task → voluntary return on another relevant occasion. Record permission/comprehension/coverage failures. Opening the popup is not activation.

## 6. Metrics and proposed thresholds

Thresholds are **planning hypotheses**, not national-demand estimates. Report counts with percentages and include failures. The primary task cohort is twelve; fewer completed sessions leave that gate unresolved rather than silently lowering its denominator.

| Measure | Denominator and window | Proposed decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment feasibility | Eligible people / people screened over 14 days | Seek ≥10 eligible participants; investigate channel/category if insufficient |
| Accurate useful answer | Completed correct actionable outcomes / all 12 assigned notebook tasks | ≥8/12, with zero critical false-equivalence; otherwise revise or narrow |
| Effort improvement | Paired completion times for tasks with preserved correctness | Median reduction ≥25%; report failed/incomplete tasks separately; no speed claim if quality worsens |
| Exact-match precision | Correct exact matches / all independently labeled proposed exact matches | Use the release benchmark gate in [testing](11-testing-and-operations.md); abstentions never inflate precision |
| Useful licensed coverage | Tasks with enough fresh eligible facts / ten preselected supported cases | ≥8/10 within the proposed ≤24-hour freshness window; test stock and missing costs separately |
| Repeat use | Voluntary reuse / participants with another relevant occasion within 30 days | Seek ≥4/8 eligible repeat occasions. Participants without another relevant occasion are excluded from this denominator and reported separately, not counted as churn. |
| Corrections and effort | Critical corrections, total corrections; founder minutes / supported case | No unresolved critical errors; investigate if median support exceeds ten minutes/case after one fix cycle |
| Later alert usefulness | Useful, current notices / delivered notices; opt-outs / recipients | NOT TESTABLE in P0; design only after an achievable alert feature is approved |

Report these four coverage breakdowns alongside task usefulness, separating synthetic, manual and permission-cleared evidence. They add diagnostic reporting; **the hypothesis thresholds above are unchanged**.

| Coverage breakdown | Numerator / denominator |
|---|---|
| Identity resolved | Offers whose intended identity is resolved from the task's evidence / all assigned offer-identification cases. Label the evidence basis; user confirmation is not independent verification. |
| Critical fields available | Report each required field separately: offers with usable evidence / assigned offers requiring that field. Include seller, warranty, stock, delivery, tax/mandatory fees, eligibility and location/payment context. Use a predeclared applicability matrix; failed retrieval remains missing. |
| Comparable alternatives | Assigned comparison cases with at least one eligible exact alternative / all assigned comparison cases. Missing decisive evidence or an unavailable alternative cannot become a successful available comparison. |
| Complete delivered total | Assigned delivered-price cases with every required same-context component established / all assigned delivered-price cases. Unknown required charges prevent completion. |

A correct abstention can count as an accurate useful answer while counting as unresolved comparison coverage. Report both outcomes; twelve correct abstentions are not twelve successful exact comparisons or complete totals.

Label a research follow-up reminder separately from natural retention. Clicks are not orders; hypothetical differences are not realized savings. This study collects no completed-purchase/counterfactual evidence sufficient to verify savings.
