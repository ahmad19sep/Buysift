# Source register

**Access date: 8 October 2026.** These 94 records include original sources, company statements, incomplete retrievals and six bounded browser-navigation observations. An entry is not a claim that every part of its page was verified. See the [evidence register](evidence-register.md) for decisions/contradictions and the [public audit](public-audit.md) for the interactive method and denominator.

Publication/update labels are recorded as displayed. They do not automatically identify an effective policy date. Data periods and retrieval times are kept explicit; “not displayed” is not an inferred date. Original publisher pages are preferred, with limitations for mirrors, search-only leads and failed retrievals. No account, installation, purchase or contract acceptance was performed.

A [machine-readable register](sources.json) contains the same reference metadata. IDs beginning M concern market/reach, D concern access, T concern platform/policy, and R concern the public browser sample. Search results are discovery aids unless an entry explicitly records a search-only limitation.
