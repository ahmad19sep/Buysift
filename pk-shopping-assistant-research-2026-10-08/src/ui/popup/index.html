How your data is handled

Offer facts, reference links, observations and valid drafts you enter are stored in this browser’s local extension storage. Example data is labeled as synthetic.

The extension does not automatically read retailer pages, monitor your browsing or send your notebook to a server. Observations older than the 90-day analysis window remain stored until you delete them and still count toward notebook limits.

Exported JSON files remain under your control. Opening a retailer link is a separate action and the retailer’s privacy policy applies there.
